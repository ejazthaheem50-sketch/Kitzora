About this free tool

Find your exact age from your date of birth in years, months, days and hours, and calculate your Body Mass Index using metric or imperial units with a clear color-coded result.

How to use it

  1. Choose your date of birth to see your exact age.
  2. Set your height and weight with the sliders, in cm/kg or ft/lbs.
  3. Read your BMI value and category: Underweight, Normal, Overweight or Obese.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. In imperial units it is 703 times weight in pounds divided by height in inches squared.

What is a normal BMI range?

For most adults a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is considered the normal range.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening number and does not measure body fat or muscle. Talk to a doctor for personal health advice.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
